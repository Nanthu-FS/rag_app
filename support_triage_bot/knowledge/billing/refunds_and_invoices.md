# Refunds, Invoices & Payment Methods

## Refund policy

- **14-day money-back guarantee** on all new subscriptions. If you cancel within 14
  days of your first payment, you receive a full refund, no questions asked.
- After 14 days, monthly plans are non-refundable for the current cycle, but you can
  cancel to stop future charges.
- Annual plans cancelled after 14 days are refunded on a prorated basis for the
  unused full months, minus any discount already applied.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5–10 business days to
  appear, depending on your bank.

To request a refund, go to **Settings → Billing → Request refund**, or reply to any
invoice email.

## Invoices

Every payment generates a PDF invoice, emailed to the billing contact and available
under **Settings → Billing → Invoice history**. You can add a company name, VAT ID,
and billing address that appear on every invoice from **Billing → Company details**.

## Payment methods

We accept all major credit/debit cards and, on Growth and Enterprise plans, ACH and
wire transfer. To update a card, go to **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails, we retry over 7 days and email you each time. Access is paused
after the 7-day grace period until payment succeeds. No data is deleted during this
window.
