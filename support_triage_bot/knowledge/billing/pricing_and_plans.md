# Nimbus Pricing & Plans

Nimbus offers three subscription tiers, billed monthly or annually. Annual billing
saves 20% compared to paying monthly.

## Plans

- **Starter** — $19/user/month (or $182/user/year). Up to 5 users, 10 GB storage,
  email support, core analytics.
- **Growth** — $49/user/month (or $470/user/year). Up to 50 users, 250 GB storage,
  priority support, API access, advanced analytics, SSO.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing. Unlimited users, dedicated storage, 24/7 support,
  SAML SSO, audit logs, a dedicated success manager, and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## Changing plans

You can upgrade at any time from **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Upgrades take
effect immediately and we charge a prorated amount for the rest of the current cycle.
Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle so you keep paid
features until then.

## Seats

Seats can be added or removed at any time. Added seats are prorated. Removed seats
are credited toward your next invoice. The minimum is 1 paid seat.

## Taxes & currency

Prices are listed in USD and exclude local taxes (VAT/GST), which are added at
checkout based on your billing address.
