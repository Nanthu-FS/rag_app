# About Nimbus, Policies & Contact

## About

Nimbus is a collaborative analytics workspace that helps teams centralize data,
build dashboards, and share insights. Founded in 2019, headquartered in Austin, TX,
with a fully remote team across 14 countries.

## Support hours

- Starter: email support, responses within 1 business day.
- Growth: priority support, responses within 4 business hours.
- Enterprise: 24/7 support with a dedicated success manager.

Business hours are Mon–Fri, 8am–8pm US Central Time. Contact: support@nimbus.io.

## Data & privacy

Your data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Nimbus is
SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. We never sell customer data. You can
export all your data at any time from **Settings → Data → Export**, and request
deletion under **Settings → Data → Delete workspace**.

## Service levels

Enterprise plans include a 99.95% uptime SLA with service credits if we miss it.
Live system status and incident history are at `status.nimbus.io`.

## Account management

Change your name, email, or notification preferences under **Settings → Profile**.
Workspace ownership can be transferred by the current Owner under
**Settings → Members → Transfer ownership**.
